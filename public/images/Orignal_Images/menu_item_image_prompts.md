# AI Image-Generation Prompts — Full Menu

Separate, self-contained prompts for every menu item, following the structure of the original King Zinger Burger demo prompt. Every prompt uses the exact same fixed studio scene, backdrop, surface, lighting setup, and camera angle (see the 'Scene & Background' line in each prompt) so the whole menu looks like one cohesive photoshoot. The brand logo is placed identically — small, top-left corner only — in every image. Replace the logo/branding reference in each prompt with your uploaded brand asset before generating.

## King Size Zinger

**Prompt:**
Create an ultra-photorealistic hero image of a premium King Size Zinger for a modern food delivery website. The burger should be the main focus, centered and captured in a high-end commercial food photography style. Showcase an oversized, extra-thick, extra-crispy golden fried chicken zinger fillet layered with fresh green lettuce, juicy tomato slices, creamy mayonnaise, and a perfectly toasted sesame seed bun. Every ingredient should look fresh, vibrant, and irresistibly delicious with realistic textures, crisp/soft detail as appropriate, and subtle steam rising where relevant.

Use soft cinematic studio lighting with realistic reflections, shallow depth of field, and a clean premium background that makes the burger stand out. Leave generous negative space around the burger for website text, pricing, and call-to-action buttons.

**Scene & Background (must be identical across every menu image):** Use this exact consistent scene setup, identical across the entire menu photo series: the dish sits on a dark matte charcoal-grey stone/slate surface, shot straight-on at a slight elevated 15-20° angle, on a smooth dark charcoal-to-black studio backdrop with a very subtle warm amber gradient glow rising from behind the dish. Lighting is soft cinematic studio lighting from the top-left with a gentle fill from the right, creating realistic reflections, soft shadows, and a shallow depth of field with a softly blurred background. No other props, no text, no changing surfaces, and no changing backdrop color between images — every dish in the series must appear to be photographed in the same physical studio location, on the same day, under the same lighting rig and camera setup, so the full menu looks like one cohesive, professionally shot collection.

Place the attached brand logo only in the top-left corner of the image, small in size (roughly 8-10% of image width), positioned as a subtle corner watermark/badge — not on the burger wrapper or packaging, not stamped on the food, and not repeated anywhere else in the frame. The logo must remain sharp, undistorted, fully legible, and an exact match to the uploaded branding, with no changes to its design or colors. The rest of the image, including the burger wrapper or packaging, must stay clean and logo-free.

The final image should look like a premium fast-food advertisement created by a professional food photographer, with 8K resolution, HDR quality, hyper-realistic textures, accurate colors, and lighting that makes the burger look fresh, delicious, and irresistible—designed to instantly increase appetite and drive online orders.

**Composition:** Single burger hero shot, centered, minimal distractions, king-size scale emphasized, clean premium aesthetic, luxury food photography, perfect for an e-commerce homepage or product page. Camera angle, distance, and framing style must match every other image in the menu series exactly. Small brand logo badge confined to the top-left corner only, same size and same position across every image; the rest of the frame stays logo-free.

**Style Keywords:** Hyper-realistic, commercial food photography, premium advertising, ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, macro photography, shallow depth of field, HDR, 8K, appetizing, luxury branding, modern website hero image.

**Negative Prompt:** Cartoon, illustration, CGI, fake textures, blurry, low resolution, distorted ingredients, duplicate ingredients, oversized vegetables, artificial colors, excessive props, cluttered background, random watermark, text artifacts, deformed food, melted/soggy bread, altered logo, oversized logo, logo covering the whole image, logo printed on food or packaging, multiple/repeated logos, logo in center or bottom of image, inconsistent background, changing backdrop color, changing surface/table, changing camera angle, changing lighting style, added props, unrealistic lighting.
